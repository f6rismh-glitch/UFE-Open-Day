Accessible Open Day

Accessibility Support for Visitors

UFE Open Day is designed so visitors with different accessibility needs can move, ask, listen, read, and participate with confidence.

On-site support

Guided access from arrival.

Visitors who need physical assistance, visual support, hearing support, cognitive guidance, or general accessibility help can be supported through trained Open Day staff.

Staff guides are briefed on respectful communication, asking before assisting, keeping pathways clear, speaking directly to the visitor, and adapting the pace of the visit when needed.

01

Mobility Access

Wheelchair ramp access, clear entry guidance, route planning, rest points, and support for visitors who may need extra time moving between areas.

02

Trained Guides

Open Day staff can act as guides for visitors with disabilities, using respectful etiquette and clear communication.

03

Digital Accessibility

The website includes larger text, high contrast, reduced motion, text-to-speech, multilingual content, and a chatbot that follows the selected language.

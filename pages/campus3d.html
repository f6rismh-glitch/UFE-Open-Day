New UFE Campus — El Shorouk
Campus Architects

Designed by Jakob + MacFarlane

Jakob + MacFarlane is a Paris-based architecture firm known for innovative, fluid designs that blend technology with organic forms.

The design for the new UFE campus reflects a vision of interconnected learning spaces, sustainability, and a campus that feels alive for the next generation of students.

Founded1997, Paris, France
ApproachParametric design, sustainability, fluid spatial experiences

Campus Vision

The new campus concept includes academic buildings, research spaces, a learning center, auditoriums, student accommodation, restaurants, administrative spaces, green areas, and parking.

Sustainability Ideas

Solar shading, greywater reuse, gardens, climate-responsive outdoor spaces, natural ventilation, and shaded walkways are reflected in the future campus story.

About this website

A digital Open Day journey built for clarity, access, and exploration.

This website is a complete student-facing Open Day platform. It connects registration, schedule planning, accessibility support, a 3D campus preview, and an interactive Pathfinder quiz into one clean experience.

01

Purpose

The site turns a university visit into a structured journey, so visitors understand where to go, what to do, and how to take the next step.

02

Design system

The interface uses UFE’s blue, white, and red identity with clean spacing, high contrast, rounded cards, and motion that feels modern without distracting users.

03

Core features

Visitors can switch languages, change theme, use accessibility tools, explore the campus model, register, and interact with the Open Day assistant.

Project logic

Everything is connected to the visitor journey.

The homepage introduces the event, the experience page explains what happens, the schedule gives structure, the 3D page creates excitement, the accessibility page builds trust, and registration turns interest into action.
